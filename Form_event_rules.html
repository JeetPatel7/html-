Event Rules for Workshop and Stalls

Welcome to our workshop event! Below are the rules and guidelines for exhibitors and attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.

1. Stall Setup and Teardown

  • Stalls must be set up 1 hour before the event starts and dismantled only after the event officially ends.
  • All materials, furniture, and equipment must be provided by the exhibitors.
  • Stalls must not exceed the allocated space (e.g., 10x10 feet).

2. Product Display

  • Only pre-approved products can be displayed and marketed at the event.
  • Exhibitors must ensure their products are displayed neatly and safely.
  • No illegal, counterfeit, or prohibited items are allowed.

3. Behavior and Conduct

  • Exhibitors and attendees must maintain a professional and respectful demeanor at all times.
  • Aggressive marketing or harassment of attendees is strictly prohibited.
  • No loud music or disruptive behavior is allowed.

4. Health and Safety

  • All exhibitors must comply with health and safety regulations (e.g., fire safety, electrical safety).
  • Emergency exits and pathways must remain clear at all times.
  • First aid kits must be available at each stall.

5. Payment and Transactions

  • Exhibitors are responsible for handling their own sales and transactions.
  • All transactions must comply with local tax and business regulations.
  • The event organizers are not responsible for any financial disputes.

6. Promotional Materials

  • Exhibitors can distribute brochures, flyers, or samples, but littering is prohibited.
  • All promotional materials must be pre-approved by the event organizers.

7. Attendee Interaction

  • Attendees are allowed to interact with exhibitors and explore products but must not damage or misuse any items.
  • Exhibitors must not force attendees to provide personal information (e.g., phone numbers, email addresses).

8. Event Timings

  • The event will run from [start time] to [end time] on [date(s)].
  • Exhibitors must remain present at their stalls during the entire event unless prior arrangements are made.

9. Waste Management

  • Exhibitors are responsible for keeping their stall area clean.
  • Trash must be disposed of in designated bins.

10. Prohibited Items

  • No hazardous materials, weapons, or illegal substances are allowed.
  • No open flames or flammable items are permitted.

11. Compliance with Organizers

  • Exhibitors must follow all instructions provided by the event organizers.
  • Failure to comply with the rules may result in removal from the event.

12. Liability

  • The event organizers are not responsible for any loss, theft, or damage to exhibitors' products or personal belongings.
  • Exhibitors are advised to secure their valuables at all times.

13. Cancellation Policy

  • If an exhibitor cancels their participation, they must notify the organizers at least [X days] in advance.
  • Refunds (if applicable) will be processed as per the event's cancellation policy.

15. Feedback and Complaints

  • Any complaints or feedback should be directed to the event organizers at the help desk.
  • Exhibitors and attendees are encouraged to provide feedback to improve future events.

Thank you for adhering to these rules. We look forward to a successful and enjoyable event!
